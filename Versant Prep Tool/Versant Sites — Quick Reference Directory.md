# Versant Sites — Quick Reference Directory

> [[00 - Index|← Back to Index]]

One-line facts on every Versant-related site found in this research (beyond Pearson itself): what it is, whether it actually helps with Versant practice, why, and one extra fact. See [[Versant Prep Sites — Walkthrough]] for hands-on detail on the four most important ones, and [[Complete Guide — English & Versant Platforms]] for the fullest write-up of every site.

---

**Pearson (official)** — the company that owns the real test. Helpful: yes, it's the source itself. Free demos and official study guides. No public pricing; companies must talk to a salesperson.

**VersantPrep** — free practice site by one independent developer. Helpful: yes. Accurate task types, real timing, explains each task before you start, scores the same way the real test does. Fully free, no card; one bug where it praised a zero score.

**MyEnglishPractice.jp** — small free toolset by one person in Japan. Helpful: yes. Covers all 5 real task types, works entirely in-browser, nothing uploaded. No login needed; maker earns a little from optional paid feedback.

**Mocko** — Canada-focused exam-prep company that recently added Versant. Helpful: yes, the most complete one found. Covers all 9 parts of one version of the real test with accurate timing per part. Honestly admits it doesn't grade pronunciation yet, only checks text; free plan exists, paid ~$20-35/mo.

**VERSANT Bridge** — paid Japanese app built just for Versant. Helpful: yes. Covers the whole test properly, well built. Subscription ~$10-24/mo, built for people in Japan specifically. Full deep-dive: [[VERSANT Bridge — Competitor Deep-Dive]].

**MockVersant** — small, free, no-signup practice site. Helpful: yes, but be careful. Accurate task timing, start practicing in seconds with no account. Falsely calls itself "OFFICIAL" — nobody knows who actually runs it.

**CA Monk** — Versant test tucked inside a bigger job-prep website for accounting students. Helpful: partially. Section split matches the real test well. Needs in-app "coins" to start the test — never enough to get in during testing.

**VersantPro** — commercial Versant prep site linked to real BPO hiring. Helpful: partially. Correctly offers two real test versions to choose from. Fake "people online now" counter and reviews that look made up.

**Unliversity** — free set of written lessons for call-center job seekers. Helpful: only for understanding the test, not practicing it. Explains all 6 parts clearly in plain language. No timer, no scoring, nothing interactive.

**Test-Prep-Guides.com** — small independent study-guide site. Helpful: a little. Free sample questions and audio clips. Nothing interactive.

**OpenExamPrep.com** — big multi-exam website with one Versant page. Helpful: a little. Very accurate facts on exact question counts and timing per real test version. Barely any real practice questions.

**Skillioma** — a "free test" that's actually a lead-capture funnel for a different business. Helpful: no. Questions don't match the real Versant format at all. Generic "Failed" result with copy-paste feedback regardless of the actual answers.

**TDS Global Solutions** — real company that sells the actual Versant test to big employers. Helpful: not as a practice tool — none exists on the site. Just a "book a sales call" page. Good proof real companies already pay for Versant-related services.

**Printed book / free PDF** — old-fashioned study book and a PDF found online. Helpful: a little. Real practice questions and answers, nothing interactive.

**PracticeTestGeeks** — unclear; blocked by a security check on every attempt to check it. Helpful: unknown. The same page title showed up copy-pasted across several different web addresses — a bad sign.

---

**In short:** about 14 real Versant-related sites exist beyond Pearson itself. The genuinely good free ones are **VersantPrep**, **MyEnglishPractice.jp**, and **Mocko**. None of them are built for a company to use for hiring — every one is built for one person practicing alone. See [[Market Gap & Positioning]].
