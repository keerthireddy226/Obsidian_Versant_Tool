# Versant Test — Official Task Structure

> [[00 - Index|← Back to Index]]

**Authoritative source:** Pearson's own *Versant 4 Skills Essential Test: Official Guide for Test-Takers* (April 2021) and *Test Description and Validation Summary* (2022), as cited in [[BRD — Versant Prep Tool]] Appendix A. **Re-verified 2026-08-25** directly against Pearson's live source PDFs (BRD Assumption A2, now closed) — every figure below (item counts, timing windows, word-count ranges, the GSE→CEFR table) was confirmed to match the current documents exactly, word for word. Both source PDFs remain unchanged since their original 2021/2022 dates. Should still be spot-checked again immediately before item generation begins, since this can change without notice.

This is the ground truth the entire product and every competitor's task-mapping is measured against.

---

## Task structure (~70 items, ~30 minutes)

| Part | Task | Items | Skill(s) scored |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Repeats | 16 | Speaking |
| B | Sentence Builds | 8 | Speaking |
| C | Conversations | 12 | Listening |
| D | Sentence Completion | 18 | Reading |
| E | Dictation | 14 | Listening and Writing |
| F | Passage Reconstruction | 2 | Reading and Writing |

An unscored 30-second spontaneous speech sample is also collected, retained for human review only — not a test item.

## Item and timing specifications

| Task | Item spec | Response window |
|---|---|---|
| Repeats | 3–15 words, increasing difficulty, conversational delivery | 15s; must begin within 6s |
| Sentence Builds | 3 phrases in random order, excluding original word order | 15s; must begin within 8s |
| Conversations | ~3 short sentences plus a comprehension question | Word or short phrase; begin within 8s |
| Sentence Completion | 5–25 words, one gap, any part of speech, sentence-initial/medial/final | 25s typed |
| Dictation | 3–14 words, heard once | 25s typed |
| Passage Reconstruction | 45–65 words | 30s read, 90s write |

## Scoring approach (Pearson's own description)

- **Repeats, Sentence Builds, Conversations:** content scored on presence or absence of expected words in correct sequences.
- **Manner of response** (fluency and pronunciation): response latency, rate of speaking, position and length of pauses, stress and segmental forms of words, pronunciation of segments within lexical and phrasal context.
- **Sentence Completion, Dictation:** expected words in correct sequences. Sentence Completion accepts all reasonable answers established in field testing.
- **Passage Reconstruction:** weighted sum of expected words and word sequences, weighted by semantic relation using a variation of latent semantic analysis; also scored for grammar, spelling, punctuation, and capitalisation.

## Construct

Facility in spoken and written English — **automaticity, not knowledge**. Strict per-item time limits are deliberate, to advantage proficient users and discriminate levels of automaticity. Items are context-independent. Memory is explicitly not the construct: every Repeat, Sentence Build, and Dictation item was validated so that at least 90% of a reference sample of educated native speakers responded correctly.

## Scales

Overall = 25% each of Speaking, Listening, Reading, Writing. Reported on Pearson's Global Scale of English (GSE), 10–90, with a corresponding CEFR level. Pearson's internal validation uses a 20–80 Versant scale. Sample score report: GSE 59/90 = Versant 57/80.

### Versant scale to CEFR (Table 7 of the validation summary)

| Versant (20–80) | CEFR |
|---|---|
| 20–23 | below A1 |
| 24–33 | A1 |
| 34–45 | A2 |
| 46–56 | B1 |
| 57–67 | B2 |
| 68–78 | C1 |
| 79–80 | C2 |

## Cross-checks from competitor research

Independent confirmations of this same structure found across the competitive landscape:
- **TDS Global Solutions** independently lists the identical 6-task, 70-question, 30-minute structure — see [[Versant Prep Sites — Walkthrough]].
- **CA Monk** arrived at a near-identical 9-category breakdown mapping cleanly onto these 6 official tasks (their "Listen and Type" = Dictation, "Sentence Construction" = Sentence Builds) — see [[Versant Prep Sites — Walkthrough]].
- **VERSANT Bridge**'s Part F strategy content (40s/response, single playback, opinion-based prompts) matches this structure's **Open Questions** task from the related Versant Speaking Test variant — see [[VERSANT Bridge — Competitor Deep-Dive]].
- **Mocko** covers all "9 parts of one version" of the real test — a different Versant product variant than the 6-task structure above (see BRD Appendix B, "Versant product variants" — plausible but unverified).

## What the current product spec covers

Per [[FRD — Versant Prep Tool]], **Phase 1 implements exactly 3 of these 6 tasks: Repeats, Sentence Builds, Dictation** — the three that don't require Reading skill coverage or semantic/open-response scoring. Sentence Completion and Conversations are deferred to Phase 2; Passage Reconstruction (the hardest to score) to Phase 3.
